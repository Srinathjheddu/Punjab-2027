Data dictionary & research discipline

Built around the Punjab 2027.xlsx research workbook — 17 tabs spanning official results, researcher-created indices and interpretation. This dashboard does not claim to forecast the 2027 result.

117 constituency recordsNames, AC numbers, 2017/2022 winners, turnout and margins, 2024 LS parliamentary-segment leads, current alignment, competitiveness and volatility.
Historical / diagnostic / inferred2017 and 2022 Assembly data are same-election-type history. 2019/2024 LS segment outcomes are secondary diagnostic evidence. 2027 class, ACI, AVI, strategic posture and issue scores are research estimates.
Regions69 Malwa, 25 Majha, 23 Doaba constituencies, assigned from the workbook. The three-region political grouping puts Puadh belt seats under Malwa, rather than using a separate fourth category.
Qualitative evidenceConstituency-specific social/issue notes exist for 20 strategic seats. Other seats show directly supported electoral signals plus clearly labeled regional context, not invented hyperlocal issues.
Seat alignmentsWorkbook “current” labels can reflect post-election joinings, bypolls or emerging blocs and can differ from formal membership or a dated official chamber tally. These must not be presented as verified live membership.
BoundariesDataMeet community AC data; CC BY 2.5 India. The map bundles 117 generalized Punjab constituency polygons from India Geodata / DataMeet S19_AC state-wise shapefiles directly in the HTML so they display without network access; the original source files remain available upstream. The bundled generalized shapes are intended for visualization, not georeferenced GIS exports. It builds regional multipolygons from matched constituency polygons. Boundaries may have shifts; verify against ECI for official use.
